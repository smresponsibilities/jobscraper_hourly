## 1. The Flagship: 'My Simple Productivity System (for normal people)!'

In his iconic viral masterclass (*"My Simple Productivity System (for normal people)!"*, video `7M6bIeVbCqA`, 2.4M+ views), Jeff rejects overly rigid systems (Tiago Forte's full PARA, Ali Abdaal's complex Notion setups) in favor of an **80/20 lightweight workflow**:

```
[ 1. CAPTURE (Frictionless) ]
  Capture every task, idea, and call within 3 seconds (Todoist / Mobile Widget).
            │
            ▼
[ 2. ORGANIZE (Actionability Over Topic) ]
  Organize by WHEN it gets done, NOT by what subject it belongs to.
            │
            ▼
[ 3. PRIORITIZE (The Rule of 3) ]
  Identify the "Daily Highlight" (1 big must-win) + 2 supporting secondary tasks.
            │
            ▼
[ 4. EXECUTE (Timeblock Focus Sprints) ]
  Block 90-minute distraction-free calendar windows; execute the 2-minute rule.
            │
            ▼
[ 5. REVIEW (The Weekly 15-Minute Reset) ]
  Clear inboxes, archive stale tasks, align with next week's strategic goals.
```

### The 5 Operational Pillars:
1. **Frictionless Capture**:
   * If capturing a task takes more than 5 seconds, you will stop doing it.
   * Use quick-add widgets on your phone home screen and keyboard shortcuts on desktop.
2. **The Organization Trap (Tiago Forte's Secret)**:
   * *The fatal mistake*: Organizing notes and tasks by category (e.g., "Marketing", "Finance", "Reading").
   * *The winning method*: **Organize by actionability**. Group tasks by project deadlines and immediate execution dates.
3. **The "Rule of 3" Daily Focus**:
   * A to-do list with 25 items is a demoralizing wish list.
   * Pick **3 tasks per day**. Once those 3 are completed, your day is an objective success. Anything else is bonus credit.
4. **The 2-Minute Rule**:
   * If an action (replying to an email, confirming a call, filing a document) takes less than 120 seconds, execute it immediately rather than scheduling it.

## 2. The 5 Golden Rules of Incredible Resumes (Google Insider Lens)

From *"Write an Incredible Resume: 5 Golden Rules!"* (4.4M+ views) and *"5 Resume Mistakes You MUST Avoid"* (2.7M+ views):

1. **Rule 1: Results > Responsibilities**:
   * Bullet points must follow the formula: `Action Verb + Context + Quantified Metric`.
   * *Example*: *"Streamlined customer onboarding pipeline, cutting cycle duration from 14 days to 4 days, boosting initial product activation by 38%."*
2. **Rule 2: The 6-Second First Impression**:
   * Clean formatting, consistent font hierarchy, zero decorative rating bars or graphic icons.
3. **Rule 3: One Page for Under 10 Years Experience**:
   * Be ruthless with editing. Remove generic coursework, high school details, and outdated tech stacks.
4. **Rule 4: Avoid the Unethical Keyword Stuffing Trap**:
   * White-text hidden keywords get flagged by modern ATS parsers (Workday, Greenhouse) and result in immediate candidate blacklisting.
5. **Rule 5: Tailor the Top 3 Bullets**:
   * Front-load the exact language of the job description into the top of your experience section.

## 3. The Perfect AI & ChatGPT Prompt Formula

In *"Master the Perfect ChatGPT Prompt Formula (in just 8 minutes)!"* (3.8M+ views):

### The 4-Part Prompt Architecture (C-T-I-O):

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ C — Context:     Who you are, who the audience is,     │
│                  and the background setting.           │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ T — Task:        The precise action to be performed    │
│                  (use strong action verbs).            │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ I — Instructions: Constraints, tone of voice, what to  │
│                  avoid, key points to emphasize.       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ O — Output:      The exact format (table, bullet list, │
│                  markdown, JSON, 3-sentence email).    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> **Example Prompt**:
> *"You are a senior tech product marketing manager at a high-growth SaaS firm (Context). Draft a follow-up email to an enterprise client after a product demo (Task). Maintain a warm, consultative, and concise tone; reference their concern about data privacy; avoid marketing fluff (Instructions). Output the response as a 3-paragraph plain text email with a clear subject line (Output)."*

## 4. Gmail & Inbox Zero: The 5-Folder Priority System

1. **`@Action Needed`**: Emails requiring a substantive response or task completion.
2. **`@Waiting For`**: Sent emails where you are blocked on another person's reply.
3. **`@Read Through`**: Newsletters, articles, and documentation to consume during downtime.
4. **Archive Immediately**: Any email read and resolved is archived instantly (never left sitting in the primary inbox).

## 5. Google Calendar Timeblocking & Focus Architecture

* **Color-Coded Energy Mapping**:
  * 🔴 High-energy deep work (writing code, designing PRDs, strategic planning).
  * 🟡 Collaborative meetings and cross-functional syncs.
  * 🟢 Administrative and communication processing (inbox zero, slack catchup).
* **Defensive Calendar Blocks**:
  * Block 9:00 AM – 11:30 AM every Tuesday and Thursday for uninterrupted focus. Treat focus blocks as unmovable meetings with yourself.
